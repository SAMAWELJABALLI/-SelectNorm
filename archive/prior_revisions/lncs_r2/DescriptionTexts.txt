1 (Fig. 1) - Block diagram of SelectNorm. Upper lane, inference: the Arabizi input x goes to the first pass of the frozen classifier and to the conservative normaliser with metadata, whose costs c0 and cT are sunk; both feed the decision features z, then the factorised benefit, then cost-aware routing. If g is 0, the first-pass prediction is returned; if g is 1, a dashed optional second pass on T(x), with avoidable cost c1, produces the prediction. Lower lane, offline: the disagreement estimator, the conditional advantage estimator, the threshold and the optional few-label recalibration.
2 (Fig. 2) - Four panels, one per test set, plotting macro-F1 against estimated inference cost for SelectNorm-adapt with tuned and Bayes thresholds, the direct-gain router B4 and confidence thresholding B3 at six values of lambda, with single points for the original view B0, always normalising B1 and the two-view ensemble R. Vertical lines mark the cost of one view and of both views.
3 (Fig. 3) - Dot plot with four panels, one per encoder, and one row per test set S3 to S6. Each row shows three AUC values with 95 percent intervals: the disagreement estimator on unlabelled target text, between 0.62 and 0.74; the advantage estimator trained on source labels, mostly between 0.54 and 0.71, with XLM-R at 0.45 and 0.44 on S5 and S6; and the target-trained cross-validated reference, between 0.53 and 0.68. A dashed vertical line marks chance at 0.5.
