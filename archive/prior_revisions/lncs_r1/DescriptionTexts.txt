1 (Fig. 1) - Block diagram of SelectNorm. Upper lane, inference: the Arabizi input x goes to the first pass of the frozen classifier and to the conservative normaliser with metadata, whose costs c0 and cT are sunk; both feed the decision features z, then the factorised benefit, then cost-aware routing. If g is 0, the first-pass prediction is returned; if g is 1, a dashed optional second pass on T(x), with avoidable cost c1, produces the prediction. Lower lane, offline: the disagreement estimator, the conditional advantage estimator, the threshold and the optional few-label recalibration.
2 (Fig. 2) - Four panels, one per test set, plotting macro-F1 against estimated inference cost for confidence thresholding, the direct-gain router and SelectNorm with tuned and Bayes thresholds, with single points for B0, B1 and the two-view ensemble.
3 (Fig. 3) - Scatter plot with one point per encoder and test corpus. The x-axis is the AUC of the disagreement estimator, between 0.62 and 0.74 for all points; the y-axis is the AUC of the conditional advantage estimator, mostly between 0.54 and 0.71, with two XLM-R points near 0.44. Dashed lines mark chance at 0.5 on both axes, and a dotted diagonal marks equal AUC. Fourteen of sixteen points lie below the diagonal.
